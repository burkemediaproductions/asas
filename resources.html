Recommended Books, Academic Studies & Articles

Books, research and expert commentary referenced by the ASAS project.

he books we reference throughout this website and in our work-book come from authors and clinicians who have spent years studying relationship dynamics, conflict patterns, and emotional abuse. To be clear, we haven’t read every book in full, however, each one offered concepts, language, and insights that helped us recognize and articulate the toxic patterns we were living through. They provided illumination and clarity, providing introductions to ideas that defined our painful experience and gave us a framework for understanding what had felt chaotic, baffling and cruel. We especially want to acknowledge Dr. Susan Heitler, whose work and guidance provided the OMG moment where we recognized we were living a textbook case of exactly what she is writing about. Her research was instrumental in helping us see the dynamics clearly and begin to unmask them with confidence.

I. Core Academic Research (Highest Authority)

1. Pathways to Adult Sibling Estrangement: Perspectives of Estranged U.S. Adults

Journal of Marriage and Family (2026)

Reczek, Torres Beltran & Crutchfield

A landmark peer‑reviewed study based on 39 in‑depth interviews. Identifies two major pathways to estrangement:

  • Family‑system drivers (parental partisanship, manipulation, favoritism)

Sibling‑behavior drivers (absent foundations, destabilizing conduct, risk containment)

This is currently the most rigorous academic work on ASAS.

2. Adult Sibling Estrangement: An Analytical Map

Amanda Harris (2026)

A clinical, systems‑based analysis of estrangement dynamics, tipping points, and the psychological logic behind withdrawal.

Highlights the 28% prevalence rate found in large sibling‑dyad studies.

II. Magazine‑Level Journalism & Expert Commentary

3. Adult Sibling Alienation: Who Does It and Why

Psychology Today – Susan Heitler, PhD (2024)

One of the few mainstream articles explicitly naming adult sibling alienation.

Explores motivations behind alienating behavior:

  • Control of caregiving
  • Control of inheritance
  • Long standing agenda
  • Drivers, competitiveness, jealousy, envy, hostility

Also connects sibling alienation to patterns seen in parental alienation research.

4. Sibling Estrangement Is Seldom an Impulsive Decision

Psychology Today – Suzanne Degges‑White, PhD (2026)

Explains why alienation victims find the most viable protective act to protect against an alienator to be complete estrangement.

Provides context on prevalence (28%) and offers insights on the emotional complexity of withdrawal.

5. Adult Sibling Rivalry (Long‑form feature)

Psychology Today – Jane Mersky Leder (1993, updated 2025)

A classic investigative piece on lifelong sibling conflict, rivalry, and distancing.

Not specifically about alienation, but foundational for understanding entrenched sibling dynamics.

III. Books Specifically Focused on Estrangement & Toxic Sibling Dynamics

These are the most directly relevant books for ASAS, based on search results.

6. Sibling Estrangement: The Rejected Sibling’s Guide to Healing Trauma, Setting Boundaries, and Recovering from a Dysfunctional Family

Elena R. Brooks

A practical guide for individuals experiencing estrangement, with emphasis on trauma, boundaries, and recovery.

7. You Are No Longer Your Sibling’s Keeper

Zelina Chinwoh

Focuses on toxic sibling relationships, conflict, and the decision to disengage.

8. The Space Between Us: A Heartfelt Guide to Adult Sibling Estrangement, Reconciliation, and Letting Go

Hannah Cole

Addresses both estrangement and the possibility of reconciliation.

9. The Estranged Sibling: A Practical Guide to Grief, Boundaries, and Peace When You’ve Lost a Brother or Sister

Cordero Gregory Patterson

Focuses on the grief and identity disruption caused by sibling loss through estrangement.

10. Bridges Burned, Bridges Built: Surviving Estrangement Between Adult Siblings

Heather James

A narrative‑driven exploration of estrangement and rebuilding.

IV. Books on Toxic Family Systems (Highly Relevant to ASAS)

These books are not exclusively about siblings, but they are essential for understanding the family‑system mechanics behind alienation.

11. Rules of Estrangement: Why Adult Children Cut Ties & How to Heal the Conflict

Joshua Coleman, PhD

A leading work on family estrangement dynamics, applicable to sibling cases.

12. Adult Survivors of Toxic Family Members

Sherrie Campbell, PhD

Provides tools for boundary‑setting and recovery from toxic family systems.

**13. Healing From Family Rifts

Mark Sichel

A classic guide to navigating and healing family estrangement.

V. Fiction That Illuminates Estrangement Dynamics (Optional but Insightful)

These novels are not academic sources, but they offer deep psychological insight into sibling conflict, estrangement, and family systems.

14. The Nest – Cynthia D’Aprix Sweeney

Explores inheritance‑driven conflict and long‑buried sibling resentments.

15. Commonwealth – Ann Patchett

A masterful portrayal of blended families, divided loyalties, and fractured sibling bonds.

16. Everything I Never Told You – Celeste Ng

Examines family secrets, parental favoritism, and sibling identity roles.
