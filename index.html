Adult Sibling Alienation Syndrome (ASAS)

Exploring toxic sibling behaviors when elders decline.

Start HereUnderstand ASAS

Our Mission

Shine a light into family systems that thrive in confusion.

Defining adult sibling alienation syndrome, offering safeguards for aging parents, and providing clear tools that identify alienators and equip families to circumvent damage and navigate trauma with informed strategies and unwavering courage.

Adult sibling alienation can begin quietly — old wounds, whispered accusations, restricted access and the influence of a determined voice. When those dynamics take hold during a parent’s decline, the consequences can reach far beyond sibling conflict.

Clarity before crisis

Learn the patterns before they become the family’s new reality.

ASAS is an educational framework, not a clinical diagnosis. It gives families and professionals language for patterns involving gatekeeping, narrative control, isolation, coercion and vulnerable-parent risk.
