Share Your ASAS Experience

Information for people who wish to privately contribute an anonymized lived experience to the ASAS educational effort.

Your Story Matters

Adult Sibling Alienation Syndrome affects individuals in deeply personal ways, yet many feel isolated or unsure how to describe what they’ve lived through. Sharing your experience can help others recognize patterns, reduce confusion, and better understand the dynamics of ASAS.

We welcome submissions from individuals who wish to contribute to this educational effort. Personal narratives offer valuable insight and help build a clearer picture of how ASAS develops, unfolds, and impacts families.

Submit Your Story

If you would like to share your experience, you may send your narrative to us privately. All submissions are handled with care and reviewed solely for educational inclusion on this site.

Please note:

  • We do not publish identifying details.
  • We cannot respond to personal situations or provide advice.
  • Submissions may be edited or declined completely based on relevance, clarity, or safety.
  • By submitting, you grant permission for your story to be reviewed and potentially be published in an anonymized format for educational and research purposes.

A Safe, Educational Space

This page exists to give voice to lived experiences while maintaining strict boundaries. Stories are used only to help others learn — never as guidance, evaluation, or commentary on any individual situation.

Your contribution, if you choose to share it, supports a broader understanding of Adult Sibling Alienation Syndrome and helps others feel less alone as they navigate their own experiences.

Educational scope

Read with context

ASAS is an educational framework. It does not diagnose or evaluate any specific person or family.
