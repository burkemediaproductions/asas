Responsible use of ASAS materials

Terms of Use

Educational use

Content is intended for general education and public awareness. It should not be treated as professional advice or as a determination about any person or family.

No diagnosis or accusation

ASAS terminology describes general patterns. Users should not use the site to publicly diagnose, defame or accuse specific people.

Research in this area continues to evolve. Content may be updated as new information and professional feedback become available.
