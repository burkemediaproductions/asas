Before you write

We cannot evaluate personal cases.

To protect privacy and maintain appropriate boundaries, ASAS does not provide personal legal, medical or therapeutic advice and cannot determine whether a specific person is an alienator.

If you are concerned about immediate abuse, exploitation, coercion or danger to a vulnerable adult, contact an appropriate local authority or qualified professional.
