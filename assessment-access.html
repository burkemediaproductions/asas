Adult Sibling Alienation Syndrome (ASAS) Professional Assessment Tool

Rate each item based on how often the behavior has occurred in the past 12 months. Rating Scale: 0 - Never, 1 - Rarely, 2 - Sometimes, 3 - Often, 4 - Very Often. If an item does not apply to your situation, select 0 - Never.

Section A — Access Restriction & Gatekeeping

A1. My sibling restricts or controls my access to our parent.

A2. My sibling interferes with my ability to communicate with our parent.

A3. My sibling withholds important information about our parent’s health or needs.

A4. My sibling delays or filters messages between me and our parent.

A5. My sibling excludes me from caregiving discussions or decisions.

A6. My sibling schedules visits or calls in ways that prevent me from participating.

A7. My sibling discourages our parent from contacting me.

A8. My sibling positions themselves as the “gatekeeper” to our parent.

Section B — Narrative Manipulation

B1. My sibling misrepresents my actions or intentions to our parent.

B2. My sibling shares selective or distorted information about me with family members.

B3. My sibling reframes past events to portray me negatively.

B4. My sibling attributes negative motives to me without evidence.

B5. My sibling exaggerates or invents problems about me.

B6. My sibling encourages our parent to distrust or fear me.

B7. My sibling presents themselves as the “reasonable” or “responsible” one in contrast to me.

B8. My sibling tells others that I am “difficult,” “unsafe,” or “unreliable.”

Section C — Triangulation & Alliance Formation

C1. My sibling pressures our parent to take sides in sibling conflict.

C2. My sibling enlists other relatives to reinforce negative views of me.

C3. My sibling discourages family members from communicating with me.

C4. My sibling portrays themselves as the victim of my behavior.

C5. My sibling uses other relatives to relay negative messages about me.

C6. My sibling forms alliances that exclude me from family decisions.

C7. My sibling encourages others to “avoid drama” by avoiding me.

C8. My sibling presents themselves as the “protector” of our parent against me.

Section D — Social & Emotional Exclusion

D1. My sibling excludes me from family gatherings or events.

D2. My sibling omits me from group messages, updates, or planning.

D3. My sibling minimizes or dismisses my role in the family.

D4. My sibling discourages our parent from expressing affection or appreciation toward me. ( ) 0 ( ) 1 ( ) 2 ( ) 3 ( ) 4

D5. My sibling creates situations where I am left out or isolated.

D6. My sibling makes decisions that affect the family without informing me.

D7. My sibling treats me as if I am irrelevant or unnecessary.

D8. My sibling encourages others to exclude me socially or emotionally.

Section E — Structural Family Impact

E1. My relationship with my parent has deteriorated due to my sibling’s actions.

E2. My sibling’s behavior has altered caregiving roles or responsibilities.

E3. Family communication patterns have changed in ways that exclude me.

E4. My sibling’s actions have caused long-term conflict or division.

E5. My sibling’s behavior has contributed to estrangement within the family.

E6. My sibling’s actions have created lasting tension or instability.

E7. My sibling’s influence has changed how the family views me.

E8. My sibling’s behavior has produced significant emotional or relational harm.
