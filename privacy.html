Website information practices

Privacy

A straightforward privacy page for this educational resource.

Information you provide

If you submit a contact form, the site may collect the information you voluntarily provide, such as your name, email address, topic and message.

Analytics and technical data

The site may use privacy-conscious analytics to understand aggregate traffic, device types and page usage. Technical logs may also be maintained for security and performance.

Personal stories and sensitive information

Please do not submit private medical records, legal documents, confidential family information or identifying case materials through a general contact form.

Third-party links

External websites have their own privacy practices. Review those policies before providing information.

Questions

Use the contact page for privacy-related questions.
