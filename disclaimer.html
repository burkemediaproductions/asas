Disclaimer & Scope of Information

Important educational, legal, medical and professional scope limitations for the ASAS website.

Purpose of This Website

This website provides educational information about Adult Sibling Alienation Syndrome (ASAS), recognized patterns of relational harm, the social, psychological, and cultural factors that contribute to alienation within families. This site is focused sharply on toxic and harmful sibling dynamics that develop when elderly parents are in decline and vulnerable. The content is intended to increase public understanding, support research, and offer general information to help clarify alienation dynamics which are often difficult and unexpected. This site will help you learn more about this phenomenon.

This site does not provide therapeutic, medical, legal, or professional advice. It is an informational resource only.

Not a Substitute for Professional Care

Nothing on this website should be interpreted as:

  • mental health diagnosis
  • psychological treatment
  • medical advice
  • legal counsel
  • crisis intervention
  • professional evaluation of any individual or family

If you are facing a situation involving abuse, coercion, mental health concerns, or legal conflict, you should consult a qualified professional who can evaluate your circumstances directly. If you have concerns about a vulnerable elderly person being isolated or controlled, contact authorities.

No Clinical or Legal Relationship

Use of this website does not create:

  • a therapist‑client relationship
  • a doctor‑patient relationship
  • an attorney‑client relationship
  • any form of professional or fiduciary relationship

The authors, contributors, and publishers of this site do not provide individualized guidance, case analysis, or personal interventions.

General Guidance Only

Any strategies, suggestions, or response options described on this site are general educational concepts and recognised patterns, not personalized advice. They may not be appropriate for all situations. Family systems involving alienation are complex, and outcomes depend on factors that cannot possibly be assessed through this website.

Readers should use discretion and seek professional support when making decisions that affect their safety, health, or legal standing.

Limitations of Information

The content on this site reflects:

  • emerging research
  • clinical observations
  • social science perspectives
  • narrative accounts
  • patterns documented across ASAS cases

Because ASAS is an under‑researched area, information may evolve as new data becomes available. The site may be updated periodically, but no guarantee is made that all content reflects the most current research at all times.

To protect privacy and maintain ethical boundaries, this website does not accept:

  • personal stories for analysis

Narrative examples included on the site are either anonymized, composite, or used with permission.
