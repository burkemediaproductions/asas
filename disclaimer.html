Disclaimer & Scope

Important boundaries for the use of this website.

Purpose of this website

This website provides educational information about Adult Sibling Alienation Syndrome (ASAS), relational harm, family-system patterns and social, psychological and cultural factors that may contribute to alienation during vulnerable-parent decline.

Not professional advice

Nothing on this site should be interpreted as mental-health diagnosis, psychological treatment, medical advice, legal counsel, crisis intervention or professional evaluation of any individual or family.

No professional relationship

Use of the site does not create a therapist-client, doctor-patient, attorney-client or fiduciary relationship.

General guidance only

Strategies and suggestions are general educational concepts. They may not fit every situation. Readers should use discretion and consult qualified professionals when decisions affect safety, health, finances or legal rights.

External resources

Links to outside publications, professionals or organizations are provided for education and convenience. Inclusion does not imply endorsement or affiliation unless explicitly stated.
